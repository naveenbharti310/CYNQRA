# 0. The Cynqra user journey

**You bring the vision. Cynqra creates the organisation that can build it.**

This is the core flow of the product. Every screen, feature and decision follows it. It was set by the founder on
29 September 2026.

**The promise:** the founder describes what they want to build, approves how Cynqra will build it, defines their own
role, and approves the workforce and budget. Cynqra executes, and the founder receives the working outcome, then
audits, refines and ships it.

The founder should not have to become an AI workforce manager. Cynqra handles the complexity. The founder gets the
outcome.

## The seven steps

1. **Describe the idea.** The founder describes what they want to build and the outcome they want to achieve.
2. **Approve the plan.** Cynqra understands the objective and presents the proposed execution plan: the workforce,
   capabilities, technology, timeline and estimated budget it needs.
3. **Define yourself.** The founder describes their background, skills, expertise and experience. Cynqra uses this
   to understand the role the founder can play in the venture, and builds the organisation again around them. A
   founder with a strong technology background, for example, does not get a CTO as a cofounder.
4. **Approve the team and budget.** Cynqra presents the final workforce and budget. The founder approves the team and
   the investment needed to achieve the objective.
5. **Watch it being built.** The AI workforce works in the background, each worker in its role, with its
   responsibilities, intelligence and objectives. The founder does not need to understand or manage any of it.
   Cynqra coordinates the workers, runs the work and orchestrates how they collaborate.
6. **Receive the working product.** When the objective is complete, the founder receives the working product and
   can download and use it.
7. **Audit, rework and refine.** If the founder is not satisfied, they can ask for an audit, say what needs to change,
   and Cynqra reworks and refines the product until it meets the original objective.

## What Cynqra is responsible for

Cynqra is responsible for delivering the objective: the working product the founder asked for, within the budget.
Whether the business succeeds is the founder's responsibility. Cynqra sells the outcome, delivered by a well
coordinated AI team. It does not sell workers.
