# RECOVERY NOTE

Read this once, act on the one open item, then forget it.

## What happened while this package was being built

On the evening of 25 August, part way through assembling this handover, the working
environment restarted and came up with an empty disk. Three minutes of uptime, nothing on
it. This was verified rather than assumed: an empty data directory, a filesystem wide
search that found none of the project files, and a machine that had clearly just booted.

The project was rebuilt from copies that had already been handed to the founder. That
recovery is the reason there is a standing rule in the M1 plan that every scored run
exports a zip the same day. The rule paid for itself within twelve hours of being written.

Worth stating for your own planning: earlier the same evening a loss was reported that
turned out to be false, an incomplete view of a healthy disk. So one false alarm and one
real failure in one evening. Treat any single environment as disposable and keep the
repository as the truth. That is the first thing to fix on your machine, and it is item 4
in the first hour list in START_HERE.md.

## What was fully recovered

The complete working tree, 145 files: the spike harness, the verifier kit, the protocol
templates, both run records with their event logs, the G0 ratification material, the
sealed S3 seeds with their scored report, and the S1 corpus with its original wording.
Nothing in `01_history/` or `02_harness/` is missing or rewritten.

The canon is recovered from the released document build, plus fresh exports of the working
pages, which are newer than any PDF.

## The one open item

Books 1 to 4 in `00_canon/` are the v0.4 PDFs. A later build, v1.2, existed with Books 1
to 4 at v0.5, and that build was lost. Book 0 is unaffected, it is v1.0 in both builds and
it is the doctrine, which is the part you must read first.

What changed between v0.4 and v0.5 was editorial: the bench review round three edits and
formatting passes. No decision, schema, contract, bar or number differs. The Decision Log
difference is already handled, because `BOOK_4_COMPANY_LIVE.md` in this folder is newer
than either PDF and carries all thirty closed decisions.

To close it: the founder has the v1.2 zip in downloads, named
`Cynqra_Production_Build_Edition_v1.2.zip`. Drop those five PDFs into `00_canon/` and
delete the v0.4 files. Two minutes of work and the folder is then complete at the highest
version that ever existed.

If that zip cannot be found, nothing is blocked. Book 0 at v1.0 plus the live Book 4 is
enough to start, and Books 1 to 3 at v0.4 differ from v0.5 only in wording.

## What was not recovered, and does not matter

A handful of one page browser reports that only restated content held elsewhere: a waiting

underlying records are all present in `01_history/`, and the counsel draft itself survives
