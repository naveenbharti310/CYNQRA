# Custom cake order tracker: product spec

## Problem
Bakery staff lose track of custom cake orders. Orders are forgotten or picked up unpaid.

## Users
Staff of a small bakery, on the shop laptop. Internal only: nothing public, no customer accounts.

## What staff can do
1. Log an order: customer name, what the cake is, pickup date (required), and an optional phone number and notes.
2. See orders due in the next few days, soonest pickup first, with overdue orders that are not picked up shown on top.
   The exact window for "the next few days" is a product rule the founder decides (task t_02).
3. Mark an order as paid. Mark an order as picked up. Each can be undone if clicked by mistake.
4. See every open order (not yet picked up), so nothing is lost even if it is outside the due window.

## Order fields
- id: assigned by the app
- customer: text, required
- cake: text, required, what to make
- pickup_date: a date, YYYY-MM-DD, required
- phone, notes: optional text
- paid: yes or no, starts at no
- picked_up: yes or no, starts at no
- created_at: when it was logged

## Constraints, stated by the founder
- Internal only, nothing public: the app listens on 127.0.0.1 only, and there is no sign up or public page.
- No card payments: "paid" is a checkbox staff tick when the customer has paid at the counter. The app never takes or stores card details.
- Runs on the shop laptop without installing anything: Python standard library only, one JSON data file, no database server, no packages.

## Out of scope
Customer notifications, pricing and invoices, reports, multiple shops, user logins.

## Success criteria
- Every custom order is logged with a pickup date.
- Staff can see the orders due in the next few days.
- Each order can be marked paid and marked picked up.
