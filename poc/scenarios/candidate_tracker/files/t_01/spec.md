# Candidate tracker: product spec

Objective: an internal tracker so two recruiters can log candidates, set a stage, and
the founder can see who is stuck.

## Users

The founder and two recruiters, inside one company. Nobody else signs in or sees it.

## Record

A candidate has a name (required), a stage, an applied on date, and an optional stuck
reason. Stages are a closed list: applied, screen, interview, offer, hired, rejected.
New candidates start at applied.

## Screens

One screen: add a candidate, change a stage, set a stuck reason, filter all, flagged,
stuck.

## Out of scope

Anything facing applicants: no careers pages, no applicant accounts. No email. One
company only.

## Open product rule

What stuck means is a product rule and goes to the founder as a decision (task t_02).
