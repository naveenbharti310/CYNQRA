# Covers forecast: design

Covers r_05.

## Screens

One screen, "Covers for the next 14 days": a table with one row per day (weekday and date, expected covers,
prep for) and a bar per row sized by the prep number. Saturday and Sunday rows are marked in a warm accent so the
busy days stand out. While the history is still the sample, a notice above the table says so.

## Flows

1. Morning check: the head chef opens the page and reads today's and tomorrow's prep numbers.
2. Weekly planning: the floor manager scans the two weeks for the busy days and staffs them.
3. End of service: the day's covers are recorded, and tomorrow's forecast uses them.
