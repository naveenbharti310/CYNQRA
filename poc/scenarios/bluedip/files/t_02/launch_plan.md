# Go-to-market plan: Bluedip

**What:** how the first restaurants start using Bluedip. **Why:** a restaurant tool is bought after it proves itself
in that restaurant. **How:** a free pilot, then a monthly subscription (r_02).

## Channels

* **Direct visits** to restaurant owners in one city, in their quiet hours, which is when they have time.
* **Owners referring owners:** restaurateurs talk to each other; each pilot restaurant is asked for two introductions.
* **Restaurant associations and their events**, to reach owners in numbers.
* **Billing-system partners, later:** once Bluedip reads covers directly from a point-of-sale system, that system's
  sales team can offer it.

## Launch

1. **Months 1 to 3: pilot.** Twenty restaurants in one city, free. Their own covers replace the sample data; each
   runs at least four offers so Bluedip learns their response.
2. **Month 4: paid launch** at the price in the financial model, with a 30-day free trial.
3. **From month 4:** forty new paying restaurants a month (assumption), from visits and referrals.

## Metrics

* Restaurants using Bluedip each week, and offers created per restaurant.
* **Estimate against result:** customers an offer was expected to bring against those it brought.
* Margin added per restaurant per month, as Bluedip estimates it: the number that justifies the price.
* Pilot to paid conversion (plan: 12 of 20), monthly churn (plan: 4%).
