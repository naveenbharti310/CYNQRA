# Market and competitor analysis: Bluedip

**What** this answers: who pays, what they use today, and where Bluedip fits (r_02). **Why** it matters: the price,
the launch and the product's first screen all follow from it. **How** to read it: **Sourced** statements say where
they come from; **Assumption** marks an estimate to test with the first restaurants.

## Customers

* **Owners of independent restaurants and small chains** in Indian cities, who see their own quiet hours every week
  and decide their own offers. Assumption: owners of 40 to 150 seat restaurants feel the afternoon gap most, because
  their staff and rent are fixed.
* **What they want:** more covers in quiet hours without training regulars to wait for discounts, and a number they
  trust before they commit.
* **Who decides:** the owner, often on a phone, between services. Assumption: a decision takes one screen or it does
  not happen.

## Competitors

* **Dining-out platforms** such as Swiggy Dineout, EazyDiner and magicpin list restaurants with discounts on the bill.
  They bring diners, and the discount is set in their marketplace. Bluedip does not compete for diners; it tells the
  owner which offer earns money, wherever it is published.
* **Restaurant billing (point-of-sale) systems** such as Petpooja report past sales. They show what happened;
  Bluedip predicts what will happen and what an offer would change.
* **Doing nothing, or a sign in the window:** the real alternative for most owners today.

Every claim about a named company is to be checked against its current product before any sales material uses it.

## Positioning

**The owner's side of the offer.** Platforms help diners find a deal; Bluedip helps the owner decide the deal: which
hours, how deep, how many customers, and what it does to margin, before it runs.

## Sources

* **Sourced, to obtain:** the National Restaurant Association of India's India Food Services Report, for the number
  and size of restaurants by city.
* No market-size figure is used in this plan. None was sourced yet, and a guessed figure would mislead the founder.
* **To collect in the pilot:** twenty owners' own hourly covers, and their answers to "what would you pay for this".
