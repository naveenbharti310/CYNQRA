# Bluedip: business brief

## Outcome

**What.** Bluedip is an app for restaurant owners. It predicts footfall and revenue for every hour and every meal
of the day, finds the quiet hours, and estimates what an offer would really do before the owner runs it: the
customers it brings, the revenue, and the margin after food cost (r_01).

**Why.** A restaurant pays rent and wages through its quiet afternoons whether or not anyone comes. Owners fill them
with discounts chosen by feel, and a discount can bring customers and still lose money, because customers who would
have come anyway also pay less. Nobody shows the owner that before the offer runs.

**How.** The owner opens Bluedip and sees today: expected covers and revenue by hour, breakfast, lunch and dinner, with
the quiet windows marked and an offer recommended for each. They can create their own limited-time offer, for
example 50% off for at most 15 customers between 1 pm and 4 pm, and Bluedip estimates it first. As the day goes,
the covers already served correct the rest of the day. Every finished offer teaches Bluedip how that restaurant's
customers respond.

Success, for the founder: a live app that predicts footfall and revenue by hour and estimates the customers, revenue
and margin an offer brings, and a checked business plan behind it.

## Priorities

1. **Honest estimates first.** Every estimate shows the assumptions behind it, and says so when it rests on a
   starting assumption rather than the restaurant's own data.
2. **Then recommendations** for each meal, including "no offer" when every discount would lose money.
3. **Release 1 is for restaurant owners only:** no diner-facing app and no payments (r_13). The owner tells diners
   about an offer in their own way; Bluedip counts who uses it.
4. **Then growth:** the pricing and funding the CFO's model shows.

## Risks

* **An estimate taken as a promise.** Estimates are labelled as expected values with their assumptions; the legal
  register covers the wording.
* **Too little data at the start.** A new restaurant's response to offers is unknown; Bluedip starts from a stated
  assumption and learns from each finished offer.
* **Deep discounts that lose money.** The CFO brings the founder one rule on how far an offer may go.
* **Owners will not pay unless it adds more margin than it costs.** The price is set against that in the financial
  model.
