# Licence and compliance register: Bluedip

The Legal and Compliance Advisor's register (r_04). **What:** the laws and contracts Bluedip and its restaurants must
respect. **Why:** an offer that misleads diners, or data handled carelessly, is a risk to the restaurant and to
Bluedip. **How to use it:** each row says where it comes from and who must confirm it. Nothing here is legal advice.

## Regulations

| # | What | Source | What Bluedip does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Offers must not mislead consumers | **Sourced:** Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and the Central Consumer Protection Authority's Guidelines for Prevention of Misleading Advertisements, 2022 | Every offer states its window, its discount and its cap; "limited" is true because the cap is enforced |
| 2 | Personal data | **Sourced:** Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 | Release 1 keeps no diner data at all; the owner's account holds only restaurant figures |
| 3 | Tax on discounted bills | **Sourced:** GST law values a supply after a discount shown on the invoice | Bluedip tells owners to show the discount on the bill; confirm with a chartered accountant |
| 4 | The restaurant's own contracts with dining and delivery platforms | **Assumption:** some contracts may restrict offering lower prices elsewhere | Onboarding asks the owner to check; Bluedip does not publish offers on any platform |
| 5 | Company registration and Bluedip's own terms of service | **Sourced:** Companies Act, 2013, for a private limited company | Before the paid launch |

## Risks

1. **An estimate read as a guarantee.** Control: estimates are called expected values, show their assumptions, and
   the terms of service say so.
2. **A restaurant's sales data leaks.** Control: data is kept per restaurant, never shared, and never sold; access is
   the owner's alone.
3. **An offer runs past its cap.** Control: the cap is enforced by the app, not left to staff.
4. **An owner breaks a platform contract with an offer.** Control: the onboarding check in row 4.

## Confirm with a professional

* **A lawyer:** the terms of service, the estimate disclaimer, and the owner's platform contracts.
* **A chartered accountant:** GST on discounted bills; the company structure.
* **A privacy lawyer:** before any release that holds diner data.
